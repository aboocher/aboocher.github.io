Grade Thresholds

Your core standards set a ceiling and a floor for your grade — you can never score better than your standards allow, but a rough percentage can only pull you down one letter below it. Your course percentage decides exactly where in that range you land. (The one exception: an A always requires actually hitting the percentage bar too — there's no floor above a B.)

Common Questions

Retake Policy & FAQ

Didn't pass a standard on the first try? That's normal — here's how to get another shot.

How do retakes work for missed standards?

Every time we have a scheduled midterm exam, you will have an explicit opportunity to retry any core or optional standards you have not yet passed, as well as during subsequent retake opportunities (e.g., office hours / designated retake sessions).

Can I retake Capstones?

No. Capstones are evaluated like traditional midterms and factored directly into your course percentage grade. Standard retake opportunities apply strictly to core and optional outcomes.
