Grading Reference

Full Grading Matrix

Your letter grade is determined by two independent measures — your overall course percentage, and how many of the 26 core standards you've passed. This page derives the exact same rule the grade estimator on the course hub uses, laid out in full.

The Rule

How Your Grade Is Set

You must clear both the course-percentage bar and the core-standards bar for a given letter grade — whichever of the two is weaker caps your grade. Concretely: compute a grade from your percentage alone, compute a grade from your core count alone, and take the lower of the two.

90%+ (or 85%+ w/ 3 optionals)
& 23+ of 26 core
B

D

F

Optional-topics boost: if you've passed 3 or more of the four optional standards (E1, E2, E3, E6), the course-percentage bar for an A drops from 90% to 85%. This only changes the top-left cell's column boundary — within the 80–89% column, 85–89% becomes an A (instead of B) when the boost applies and you still have 23+ core standards; 80–84% stays a B either way. It has no effect on the B/C/D rows.
