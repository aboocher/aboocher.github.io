How Persistence Works

Why LocalStorage? Browser security prevents static websites (like standard GitHub Pages) from automatically reading cookies or databases across different physical devices (e.g., phone vs desktop) without a user login server.

How to sync across your phone & laptop:
  1. Use the tracker on your primary device.
  2. Click Sync / Export and download your JSON file.
  3. Send the JSON file to your second device and click Upload JSON Backup!

Your data auto-saves in your browser's local cache every time you click the BENNET button!
