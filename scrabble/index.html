🀄 Scrabble Practice Games

A collection of tools for drilling word knowledge — hooks, bingos, stems, and probability.

🎯

Blank Bingos

Find every bingo hiding behind a blank tile, from your own word list or a preset one.
💡

Words With Hints

Solve 7-letter anagrams with a hint system, ordered by how common each rack is.
🪝

Hooks

Spot which words take a front or back hook for a chosen letter — NWL or CSW.
🌱

Stem Recognition

Extend 6- and 7-letter stems to all their valid 7s and 8s.
🔤

-ING / -Y Practice

Pick out which words also take an S after -ING, or an EY after -Y.
🔍

Probability Lookup

Look up any 7- or 8-letter word's rarity rank among all possible racks.
📊

Word Value Table

Compare exact draw probabilities between words, with 0, 1, or 2 blanks.
