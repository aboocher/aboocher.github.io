-ING / -Y Practice

  • You'll get a grid of 25 words and a question. The answer is "YES" for somewhere between 4 and 7 of the words.
  • Just want to drill what the "YES" words look like? Turn on Easy Mode — then the answer is YES for 24 of the 25 words, with one exception.
  • In Easy Mode, turning on the hint shows the first letter of that lone exception.
